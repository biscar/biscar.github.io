Privacy Policy

Tidemerge: Aquarium Worlds (package com.biscar.tidemerge) · Last updated: 2026-11-20 · Русская версия

Who we are

The game is published by biscar ("we"). Contact: stukov1989@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game has no accounts and no server of our own. Your progress, settings and language are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game or using "Reset" in Settings removes them.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads (rewarded videos and interstitials) through the Appodeal mediation SDK, which connects ad networks. To show and measure ads, Appodeal and the ad networks it works with may collect and process your advertising ID, IP address, device and OS information, approximate region, app version and your interactions with ads. This data is used to serve ads, to limit how often you see them, to prevent fraud and to measure results.

Appodeal's own policy: https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy. See also the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent to personalized ads (Google User Messaging Platform, built into the Appodeal SDK) before ads are requested. You can decline; you will then see non-personalized ads.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to count anonymous usage events, such as reaching a milestone in the game or watching a rewarded ad, so we can improve the game. These events are linked to an app instance identifier, not to your name or contact details.

Notifications

With your permission the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, that your offline income is ready). They are created by the app on the device; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in Android settings.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, precise location, contacts, photos or microphone, and we do not sell personal data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (16 in some countries) and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data through the game, contact us and we will help remove it with the relevant services.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. We hold no personal data ourselves; for data held by Appodeal, ad networks or Google, contact them using the links above, or write to us at stukov1989@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
